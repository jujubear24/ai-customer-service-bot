# Refund Policy

    **Last Updated: December 2025**

## Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with our service. This policy outlines when and how you can request a refund.

## 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

### Eligibility

Our 30-day money-back guarantee applies to:

- First-time Pro plan subscribers
- Annual plan purchases (first year only)

### How to Request

1. Contact support@example.com within 30 days of purchase
2. Include your account email and reason for refund
3. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days

### Exclusions

The 30-day guarantee does NOT apply to:

- Renewal payments (only first-time purchases)
- Enterprise plans (handled per contract)
- Add-on purchases (API overages, additional storage)
- Accounts terminated for Terms of Service violations

## Prorated Refunds

### Downgrades

When downgrading from a higher tier:

- Annual plans: Credit applied to your account for unused months
- Monthly plans: No refund; downgrade takes effect at next billing cycle

### Cancellations

- **Monthly plans**: No refund for current month; access continues until period ends
- **Annual plans**: Prorated refund for remaining full months if requested within 90 days

## No Refund Situations

We cannot offer refunds in these cases:

- Account banned for Terms of Service violations
- Chargebacks initiated with your bank (contact us first!)
- Requests made more than 90 days after purchase
- Purchases made with promotional credits or gift cards
- Enterprise contracts (refer to your agreement)

## Enterprise Customers

Enterprise refunds and credits are governed by your specific contract terms. Please contact your Account Manager or email enterprise@example.com for refund requests.

## Processing Time

- **Credit card refunds**: 5-7 business days
- **PayPal refunds**: 3-5 business days
- **Bank transfer refunds**: 7-10 business days

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Credits vs. Refunds

In some cases, we may offer account credits instead of refunds:

- Partial month calculations
- Service interruption compensation
- Goodwill gestures

Credits never expire and can be applied to future purchases.

## Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision:

1. Reply to your support ticket with additional context
2. Request escalation to a supervisor
3. Contact billing@example.com for final review

We aim to resolve disputes within 10 business days.

## Contact Us

For refund requests or questions about this policy:

- **Email**: support@example.com
- **Subject line**: "Refund Request - [Your Account Email]"
- **Response time**: Within 24 hours (business days)
