# General FAQs

## Account Management

### How do I create an account?

To create an account, visit our website and click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner.
You'll need to provide your email address, create a password, and verify your email. The entire process takes less than 2 minutes.

### How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. We'll send you a secure link to reset your password. The link expires after 24 hours for security reasons.

### Can I change my email address?

Yes, you can change your email address in your account settings. Go to Settings > Profile > Email Address. You'll need to verify your new email before the change takes effect.

### How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note this action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed within 30 days as per our data retention policy.

## Subscription & Pricing

### What subscription plans do you offer?

We offer three plans:

- **Free**: Basic features, limited to 100 requests per month
- **Pro** ($19/month): Unlimited requests, priority support, advanced features
- **Enterprise** (custom pricing): Dedicated support, custom integrations, SLA guarantees

### How do I upgrade my plan?

Go to Settings > Subscription > Upgrade. Select your new plan and enter payment details. Your upgrade takes effect immediately, and we'll prorate the charge for the current billing period.

### Can I get a refund?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for first-time Pro subscribers. Enterprise refunds are handled according to your contract terms. Contact support@example.com for refund requests.

### Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or educational institutions?

Yes! We offer 50% off Pro plans for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact sales@example.com with proof of status to apply.

## Service Features

### What file formats are supported?

We support PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, and XML files. Maximum file size is 25MB for Free users and 100MB for Pro users.

### Is there an API available?

Yes, our REST API is available for Pro and Enterprise users. Documentation is at docs.example.com/api. API access includes 10,000 requests per month for Pro and unlimited for Enterprise.

### Can I use this service offline?

Our service requires an internet connection. However, Pro users can download reports for offline viewing in PDF format.

### What languages are supported?

Our service supports English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Chinese. More languages are coming soon!

## Security & Privacy

### Is my data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

### Who can see my data?

Only you can access your data. Our support team can only view metadata (not content) unless you explicitly grant access for troubleshooting.

### Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in AWS data centers in your selected region (US, EU, or Asia-Pacific). Enterprise customers can specify additional data residency requirements.

### How long do you retain my data?

Active account data is retained indefinitely. Deleted account data is purged within 30 days. Backup data is retained for 90 days for disaster recovery.
