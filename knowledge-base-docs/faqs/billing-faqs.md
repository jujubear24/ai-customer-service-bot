# Billing FAQs

## Payment Methods

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

- Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards with Visa/MasterCard logos
- PayPal
- Bank transfers (Enterprise plans only)

### Can I pay with cryptocurrency?

We do not currently accept cryptocurrency payments. We may add this option in the future based on customer demand.

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes. We use Stripe for payment processing and never store your full card details. All transactions are encrypted with TLS 1.3 and we're PCI DSS compliant.

## Billing Cycles

### When am I charged?

- **Monthly plans**: Charged on the same date each month as your signup date
- **Annual plans**: Charged once per year on your signup anniversary

### Can I change my billing date?

Monthly billing dates cannot be changed. Annual plan customers can contact support to adjust their renewal date.

### What happens if my payment fails?

If payment fails:

1. We'll retry the charge after 3 days
2. You'll receive email notifications
3. After 3 failed attempts over 9 days, your account downgrades to Free
4. No data is deleted—upgrade to restore Pro features

## Invoices & Receipts

### How do I get an invoice?

Invoices are automatically emailed after each payment. Access all invoices in Settings > Billing > Invoice History.

### Can I add my company details to invoices?

Yes! Go to Settings > Billing > Company Details to add:

- Company name
- Tax ID / VAT number
- Billing address

Changes apply to future invoices only.

### Do you provide tax invoices for VAT?

Yes, we provide VAT-compliant invoices for EU customers. Enter your VAT number in Settings > Billing to receive proper tax documentation.

## Plan Changes

### How do I upgrade my plan?

Go to Settings > Subscription > Upgrade. Select your new plan and confirm. Upgrades take effect immediately.

### How much does upgrading cost?

When upgrading mid-cycle:

- We calculate the prorated cost for remaining days
- You're charged the difference immediately
- Your new billing date remains the same

### Can I downgrade my plan?

Yes. Go to Settings > Subscription > Downgrade. When downgrading:

- Change takes effect at the end of your current billing period
- You keep Pro features until then
- No partial refunds for monthly plans

### What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your data is preserved, but:

- Files over Free limits become read-only
- API access is disabled
- Advanced features are locked

Upgrade again anytime to restore full access.

## Enterprise Billing

### How does Enterprise billing work?

Enterprise customers receive:

- Custom invoicing (monthly, quarterly, or annual)
- Purchase order support
- Net 30 payment terms
- Dedicated billing contact

### Can Enterprise plans pay by invoice?

Yes. Enterprise plans support:

- Bank transfer / wire
- Check payments
- Purchase orders

Contact your Account Manager to set up invoice billing.

### Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes, Enterprise plans include volume pricing. Discounts are based on:

- Number of users
- Contract length
- Payment terms (prepay discounts available)

Contact sales@example.com for a custom quote.

## Cancellation

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. You'll keep access until the end of your paid period.

### Is there a cancellation fee?

- **Monthly plans**: No cancellation fee
- **Annual plans**: No fee, but no refund unless within 90 days (prorated)
- **Enterprise**: Per your contract terms

### What happens after I cancel?

After cancellation:

- Your account converts to Free tier
- Data is preserved for 90 days
- After 90 days, data may be deleted per our retention policy
- You can resubscribe anytime
